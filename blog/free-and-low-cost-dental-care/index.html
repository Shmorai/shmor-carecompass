Free and low-cost dental care: where to look

Millions of Americans have no dental coverage. If cost is stopping you from seeing a dentist, these options can help.

By the CareCompass editorial team · 24 September 2026 · Updated 15 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Mariia Belinska on Unsplash

Where to find affordable care

  • Community health centers: federally funded health centers offer dental care with sliding-scale fees based on income. Search at FindAHealthCenter.hrsa.gov.
  • Dental schools: students treat patients under supervision, often at reduced cost. Appointments can take longer.
  • Dental hygiene schools: low-cost cleanings.
  • Medicaid and CHIP: children’s dental is covered; adult coverage varies by state.
  • Free clinic events: charitable organizations and state dental associations run free dental days.
  • Clinical trials: sometimes offer treatment at no cost.

Tips to reduce costs

  • Ask for the practice’s cash price and any in-house membership plan.
  • Ask for a Good Faith Estimate before scheduled treatment.
  • Prioritize: treat pain and infection first, then plan the rest.
  • Consider a dental savings plan.

When it cannot wait

Swelling, fever or spreading infection needs urgent care. See our emergency dentist guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get free dental care without insurance?
Sometimes. Free clinic events, some community health centers and charitable programs offer free care, and dental schools offer reduced-cost care.
