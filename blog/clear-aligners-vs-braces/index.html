Clear aligners vs braces: which is right for you?

Both clear aligners and braces can straighten teeth. The right choice depends on your bite, your lifestyle and how disciplined you will be.

By the CareCompass editorial team · 23 September 2026 · Updated 15 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Harold Hisona on Unsplash

How each works

Braces use brackets bonded to the teeth and wires adjusted by the orthodontist. Options include metal, ceramic (tooth-colored) and lingual braces placed behind the teeth. Clear aligners are a series of removable plastic trays, each moving teeth slightly, usually worn for most of the day.

Cost and coverage

Cost depends on case complexity and treatment length more than on the appliance. Dental plans with orthodontic benefits may cover part of either, often up to a lifetime maximum, and HSA/FSA funds can be used. Frequently asked questions

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?
For many mild to moderate cases, yes, when worn as prescribed and planned by an experienced provider. Complex bites may be better treated with braces.
