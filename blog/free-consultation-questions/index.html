Free consultation checklist: 15 questions to ask

A free consultation is your chance to interview the provider. Take this checklist with you, and do not be afraid to ask everything.

By the CareCompass editorial team · 11 October 2026 · Updated 15 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Towfiqu barbhuiya on Unsplash

About the provider

  1. What is your training and certification for this procedure?
  2. How many of these procedures do you perform?
  3. Can I see before-and-after photos of your own patients?
  4. Where will treatment take place, and is the facility accredited?

About cost and insurance

  1. Is this consultation completely free? Are X-rays or scans included?
  2. What is the total cost, itemized in writing?
  3. Are you in-network with my plan, and will you submit a pre-treatment estimate?
  4. What payment plans or financing do you offer, and what are the terms?
  5. What happens to the cost if I need a revision or redo?

Check your coverage in our insurance guide.

About the treatment

  1. What are my options, including non-surgical or lower-cost alternatives?
  2. What results can I realistically expect?
  3. What are the risks and complications?
  4. What is the recovery time, and when can I return to work?
  5. How many follow-up visits are included?
  6. Who do I contact if something goes wrong after hours?

Frequently asked questions

Should I bring anything to a consultation?
Bring your insurance card, a list of medications and health conditions, any recent X-rays or records, and your list of questions.
