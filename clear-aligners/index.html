In-office vs direct-to-consumer aligners

Aligners prescribed and monitored in person by an orthodontist or dentist allow X-rays, regular check-ins and adjustments. Mail-order aligner models with less in-person supervision have been around for years, but the American Association of Orthodontists recommends an in-person exam before starting treatment. See our detailed comparison.

Insurance for orthodontics

Many dental plans include orthodontic coverage for children under a set age, often with a separate lifetime maximum; adult orthodontic coverage is less common. Medicaid and CHIP may cover medically necessary orthodontics for children. HSA and FSA funds can usually be used for braces and aligners.

Frequently asked questions

Do orthodontists offer free consultations?
Many do. The first visit often includes an exam and treatment options; ask whether X-rays or scans are included.
Am I too old for braces?
No. Adults can have orthodontic treatment as long as their teeth and gums are healthy.
