Healthcare in the US, made easier to navigate

American healthcare is famously hard to navigate: different plans, networks, deductibles and fine print, and prices that are rarely shown up front. CareCompass is an independent guide that does the groundwork for you. We explain treatments in plain English, show you which questions to ask, and point you to official sources such as Medicare.gov and HealthCare.gov.

We focus on the care people most often research before they book: dental care, implants, orthodontics, cosmetic surgery, injectables and private and concierge medicine.

Free consultations, honestly explained

Many dental, orthodontic and cosmetic practices offer a free first consultation, so you can meet the provider, talk through options and get a written treatment plan before you commit. What is included varies: X-rays, 3D scans and exams are sometimes charged separately. Our free consultation guide explains what to expect and how to avoid surprise bills.

All insurance welcome here

Whether you have an employer PPO, an HMO, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, CHIP, a Marketplace plan, a dental savings plan or no insurance at all, CareCompass has a guide for you. Our insurance hub explains what each type usually covers, and how to confirm a provider is in-network before your appointment.

CareCompass is not an insurer or a medical provider. Always confirm coverage with your plan and network status with the practice before treatment.

Frequently asked questions

Is CareCompass free to use?
Yes. CareCompass is free for patients. We are part of the Shmor.ai network and may earn a commission from some partner links, at no extra cost to you.
Do you accept my insurance?
CareCompass is not a clinic, so we do not bill insurance. Instead we explain every common plan type (PPO, HMO, Medicare, Medicaid, CHIP, Marketplace and dental plans) and show you how to find providers in your network.
Are the consultations really free?
Many practices offer a free first consultation for elective and cosmetic care, but terms vary. Always ask whether X-rays, scans or exams are included before you book.
